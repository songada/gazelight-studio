Questions

Before you book.

How do I book?

Email song@gazelight.studio with the package and a couple of dates. We confirm by email — that is the whole booking flow. There is no online calendar or payment page on the site yet.

What do I get after the session?

A private online gallery with your retouched photos, ready to review, share and download. The gallery stays online for one year from your session date.

How many photos do I actually get?

The number on your package is the number of retouched photos included: Mini 5, Classic 10, Signature 35, Outdoor 20–30, Graduation Studio 10 / Campus 25+. Classic's 10 is a cap — want more? Add retouched photos at $30 each.

Can my dog come?

Yes — pets join family sessions free, and we have a whole Pets gallery to prove it. Tell us in your booking email so we set up for them.

What should we wear?

Solid, coordinated colours photograph best — avoid busy logos and neon. We will send a short prep note after you book, including backdrop choices.

Do you offer prints?

Yes — prints start at $10 (5×7) and $25 (8×10), ordered from your gallery. Your digital files are always included, so prints are optional.
